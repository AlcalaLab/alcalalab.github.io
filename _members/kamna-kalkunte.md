Kamna is a third-year at William and Mary studying Biology and Computational and Applied Mathematics and
Statistics (CAMS). She is interested in quantitative and computational methods to answer questions about
biological diversity and ancient gene transfers. Kamna's work in the lab focuses on gene transfers involving
Foraminifera, a diverse class of protists. In her free time, Kamna likes to watch movies, dance, and spend time
with friends.
